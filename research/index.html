Research

My research aims to understand the genomic basis of adaptation in natural populations. During my PhD I mapped the action of natural selection across the Drosophila melanogaster development and anatomy following a multi-omics data integration approach, and developed methods to estimate adaptation such as the integrative McDonald and Kreitman test (iMKT). As a postdoc, I studied the interplay between transposable elements, epigenetic marks and gene expression in natural populations of D. melanogaster.

Research interests: population genomics · natural selection and adaptation · transposable elements · epigenomics · evo-devo · bioinformatics
