# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-microscope" %}Research

My research aims to understand the genomic basis of adaptation in natural populations.
During my PhD I mapped the action of natural selection across the _Drosophila melanogaster_ development and anatomy following a multi-omics data integration approach, and developed methods to estimate adaptation such as the integrative McDonald and Kreitman test (iMKT).
As a postdoc, I studied the interplay between transposable elements, epigenetic marks and gene expression in natural populations of _D. melanogaster_.

**Research interests:** population genomics · natural selection and adaptation · transposable elements · epigenomics · evo-devo · bioinformatics
