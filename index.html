Marta Coronado-Zamora

Marta Coronado-Zamora Lecturer Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona

I am a Lecturer in Genetics at the Department of Genetics and Microbiology of the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (UAB).

I am interested in how natural populations adapt to their environment. My work combines population genomics, transposable elements, epigenomics and multi-omics data integration, mostly in Drosophila melanogaster, and more recently in other species such as mosquitoes and ticks.
